# Finlandia Hall facade candidate

This texture-only reconstruction is restricted to City of Helsinki building `BID_a1212feb-336e-4d01-a439-dada4d89d759`, VTJ `1032092665`, Mannerheimintie 13e. The chart catalogue pins 124 exact flat wall charts on pages 1006, 1008, 1011, 1013 and 1016 of the expanded city model. All 14 building pages (1005–1018) were inspected. The atlas uses the existing 903-page prefix; no removed 2017 mesh is involved.

The primary architectural reference is the four-elevation NRT drawing dated 30 December 2020 in [Helsinki planning document ak12678, PDF page 61](https://www.hel.fi/hel2/ksv/liitteet/2022_kaava/ak12678_selostus.pdf). The [architect's exterior photo gallery](https://www.arkkitehditnrt.fi/toitamme/finlandia-talo), [Aalto Foundation building reference](https://www.alvaraalto.fi/en/architecture/finlandia-hall/), Finlandia Hall's congress photo and 2005 building-history survey were opened as additional visual evidence. Exact URLs, file hashes, credits and attempted Street View access are in `source-ledger.json` and the evidence reference ledger. Both sanctioned browsers were unavailable; no Street View was viewed.

Production pixels use only this building's licensed raw city marble, granite and glazing colour/material donors, plus original authored joints, frames and simplified glazing. Reference photographs and drawings are not production pixel inputs. Panel dimensions, frame widths and glass interiors are approximate; this is not a surveyed facade reconstruction. The northern stair, clean source window bank and clear congress upper glazing are retained. Reconstructed windows/entrances have explicit intervals in `wall-mapping.json`; they are not inferred by a general foliage-colour filter.

The southern end-wall chart **1016-18** was resolved further using the site plan and first/second-floor plans (PDF pages 55, 57–59), which show one central upper opening group and two lower entrance positions. Its central upper bank is preserved at along-wall coordinate `[1024.6,1031.2]`, runtime Y `[2.2,4.95]`. Expanded lower entrance columns `[1017,1019.6]` and `[1024.3,1027.1]`, Y `[-5.7799,1.7]`, remain original source pixels; no hidden doors are fabricated. Native `runtime-views-v2` exposed photographed tree remnants in the narrow Y `[1.7,2.2]` band above them. This band now uses the same licensed page 1013/chart 1013-0 granite donor as the adjacent plinth. Final ground-only HCOL samples at 0.05 m outside the wall have minima 1.80011 m and 2.09368 m, burying the reconstruction bottom by at least 0.10 m. The upper bank, source geometry and all lower uncertain entrance pixels stay preserved. The chart's full runtime XZ bounds are X `[-1013.0570,-985.1099]`, Z `[-615.3037,-613.8524]`. Exact source UV corners and final support samples are in `data/evidence/district-expansion-20261018/visible-base-materials-cpu-v1.json`; native compressed review is required after this material correction.

The implementation is `tools/world/lib/finlandia-facade-restoration.ts`; tests are in the corresponding world test file. This worker did not register the module or change any built cells, terrain, geometry or collision. CPU inspection previews are in `data/work/district-expansion-20261018/finlandia-facades/`, with `elevation-west.png`/`elevation-west-after.png` showing the clearest full-building comparison. Registration and runtime verification belong to the integrating build.
