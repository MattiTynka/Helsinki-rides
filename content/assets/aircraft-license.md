## Adaptations

Both models were normalized in scale and coordinates and fitted to runtime hubs,
seats and collision envelopes. Legacy helicopter materials were baked to web
materials; helicopter meshes were simplified while preserving the main blades.
Textures were resized and palette encoded with metadata removed, and buffers
were compressed with lossless Meshopt storage compression. Source URLs, creators,
licenses and modifications must remain credited when redistributing the assets.

These third-party assets are excluded from the repository's MIT license grant.
