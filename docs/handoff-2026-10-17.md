### Register stairs (requests 1 and 3)

The grand stairs were misaligned because their OSM chord runs about 6° off the real tread lines. The tread lines
follow the city's street-part polygon (YLRE 460008) and the terrain contours, and the west end met the retaining
wall.

`registerFlights` in `tools/world/lib/ground.ts` builds straight flights instead:

- it takes YLRE `Portaat` polygons, grouped, and fits a plane to the terrain under them;
- treads run along the polygon edge nearest the uphill direction;
- the step count comes from a crossing OSM steps way, else rise / 0.16 m;
- flights with risers under 8 cm are dropped as shallow.

`build_cells.ts` drops OSM flights that a register flight replaces or overlaps. Options and the note are in
`content/helsinki/cells.json` `registerStairs`.

### Painted-tree erase rework (request 4, the open defect)

`tools/world/lib/vegetation.ts`, called from `build_cells.ts`'s atlas `prepare` hook.

**Mask.** A wall texel is a candidate when its line to an oblique camera (30–60° elevation, sideways spread
0.6) passes through a register tree's crown cylinder.

**Segmentation, rewritten:**

- foliage-coloured seeds inside the mask (needs saturation ≥ 0.15, so green-grey plaster stays);
- connected growth through foliage within `reachMetres`;
- closing, enclosed-hole fill, a dark rim for shaded leaves, then a 2-texel seam.

**Fill, rewritten:**

- texels are never copied across charts (UV islands);
- the bay period comes from row autocorrelation (≥ 0.6);
- whole-bay shifts are tried, best first, by kept coverage × lightness;
- whatever is left gets a push-pull fill per chart.

The row-median fill, which made the streaks, is gone.

Calibration (`data/evidence/handoff-20261017/calib5`, `calib6`; columns before | mask | after) shows it is
**still not acceptable**:

- `calib6/t28` row 2: with `reachMetres` 15 (current `cells.json`) the growth follows the crowns over the
  whole chart and wipes the National Library colonnade to a grey smear. `calib5` used 4 m and cut crowns along
  the reach boundary instead.
- `calib6/t28` row 1: dark speckles of shadow remain between the University pilasters.
- `calib6/t56`: per-chart push-pull leaves blurred grey-beige blobs on glass and shadowed walls. The yellow
  bleed from `calib5` is gone.
